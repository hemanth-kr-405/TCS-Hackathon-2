# TCS Retail Payment & Billing FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What payment methods are accepted?
We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Net Banking, UPI, and TCS Retail Gift Cards.

### Q2: Why was I charged twice for an order?
Double charges usually represent a temporary pre-authorization hold by your card issuer. If two actual charges post to your account, our support team can instantly verify transaction IDs and issue a duplicate charge reversal within 24 hours.

### Q3: Is cash on delivery (COD) available?
COD is available on orders under $300 for select pin codes with a standard verification OTP.
