# TCS Retail Delivery & Shipping Policy

## Shipping Modes & Delivery Timeframes
TCS Retail offers multiple delivery tiers depending on location and shipping preference:

* **Standard Delivery**: **3 to 5 business days**. Free on orders above $50.
* **Express Delivery**: **1 to 2 business days**. Available for select zip codes.
* **Same-Day Express**: Order before 12:00 PM local time for evening delivery in metro areas.

## Delivery Delays & Tracking Overdue
* If an order shows no tracking updates for over **48 hours** after shipment, or is **2+ days past the estimated delivery date**, our system automatically marks it for logistics priority tracing.
* Customers can check live status by providing their 5-digit Order ID (e.g. `45821` or `10092`).
* In the event of confirmed parcel loss by carrier (FedEx/BlueDart), TCS Retail provides immediate **free express re-shipment** or a **100% full refund**.
