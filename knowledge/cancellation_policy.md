# TCS Retail Cancellation Policy

## Order Cancellation Rules
Orders can be cancelled or modified within **2 hours** of placement directly from the 'My Orders' section or via customer support chat, provided the status is still **Processing**.

### Shipped / In-Transit Orders:
* Once an order transitions to **Shipped** or **Out for Delivery**, it cannot be cancelled while in transit.
* Customers can refuse delivery at the doorstep or initiate a 30-day return upon arrival for a full refund.
* Cancelled orders processed prior to dispatch receive a **100% full refund** within 24 hours.
