# TCS Retail Refund Policy

## Refund Timelines & Method
Refunds are processed automatically once the returned parcel is received and inspected at our fulfillment center or scanned by the logistics carrier.

### Timelines by Payment Method:
* **Credit / Debit Cards**: **3 to 5 business days** after carrier first-scan confirmation.
* **Store Credit / Digital Wallet**: Instant processing within **24 hours**.
* **Net Banking / UPI**: **2 to 3 business days**.

### Shipping Charges Refundability:
* Original shipping fees are non-refundable for standard returns.
* Full refund including shipping charges is guaranteed if the return is due to **damaged goods**, **wrong product delivered**, or **defective hardware**.

### Delayed Refund Escalation:
If a refund does not reflect after 7 business days, customers can contact TCS Customer Support with their return tracking number for immediate supervisor override and manual disbursement.
