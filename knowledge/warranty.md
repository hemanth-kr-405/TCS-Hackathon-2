# TCS Retail Warranty Policy

## 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty
All electronics, wireless headphones, laptops, and smart appliances purchased through TCS Retail carry a **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty**.

### Warranty Coverage:
* Hardware defects, internal component failures, power/charging issues, and bluetooth connectivity flaws.
* Firmware or battery degradation issues (e.g., rapid battery drain post firmware update) receive free diagnostic inspection and replacement.
* If repair takes more than **5 business days**, a brand-new factory sealed replacement unit is issued immediately.

### Exclusions:
* Accidental physical damage (cracked screen from fall, liquid damage) unless an extended protection plan was purchased.
