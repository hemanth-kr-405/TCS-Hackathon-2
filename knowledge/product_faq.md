# TCS Retail Product Support FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How do I request a size replacement for apparel/shoes?
Navigate to 'My Orders' -> 'Request Exchange', select the new required size, and download your instant prepaid return shipping label.

### Q2: What should I do if my headphone battery drains rapidly?
Reset your headphones by holding the power button for 10 seconds. If the issue persists after firmware v2.1, our 1-year warranty covers an immediate product exchange.

### Q3: Are damaged items replaced for free?
Yes! If fragile items like glassware or electronics arrive broken or damaged in transit, upload a photo in chat or via email to receive a replacement dispatch within 24 hours.
